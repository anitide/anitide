Tools for keeping up with anime and manga

AniTide is the umbrella for the projects listed below.

The projects are the actual work, and AniTide is just the name over them. Each one is free to use, built and run in the open, and stays small enough to do its one job properly.

Below are the projects we maintain under that name.

AniTrack

AniTrack is a free anime airing tracker. It turns your watchlist into an airing schedule and delivers release notifications to a web inbox or to Discord. Imports from AniList and MyAnimeList are supported, so there is no list to rebuild by hand.

Mangasurf Coming soon

Mangasurf is a free manga reader built around getting the chapter in front of you quickly, on a phone or a desktop. It reads from MangaDex and other sources, so a series that is missing on one of them is still there to open, and you stay in the same reader either way.
